Read the rhythm

Every burst represents one selected launch. The horizontal axis is the calendar, moving at constant speed; each rocket climbs straight up from its release date and bursts at the launched model's Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) score, so the bursts trace capability rising over time. A step line per lab follows its best score so far. Launches that Epoch AI has not scored spray a low fountain on the date axis and show “—” instead of a number. The burst and its sound coincide with the release date; trajectories begin 0.36 seconds earlier. Each lab keeps its own burst shape and marker (circle, diamond, square). Each lab's list shows the newest name on top with its score: an arrival slides in and pushes older names down, and each name stays among the four newest for at least 1.4 seconds. An asterisk and hollow marker identify previews.

Capability scores

Burst heights use the Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) by Epoch AI, licensed CC BY 4.0, retrieved 2026-10-03 (CSV). 45 of 51 selected launches have a score. ECI combines many benchmarks onto one scale, and Epoch publishes an uncertainty interval several points wide for each model, so small gaps are not rankings. When an event groups several tiers, the table names the scored model. This visualization is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epoch AI.
