Read the rhythm

Every burst represents one selected launch. The horizontal axis is the calendar, moving at constant speed; each rocket climbs straight up from its release date and bursts at the launched model's Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) score, so the bursts trace capability rising over time. The camera never shakes or moves to the beat: it glides with the calendar and the newest scores, tightens slowly in stages as the music builds, and pulls back once to the whole chart at the observation cutoff. A step line per lab follows its best score so far. Beside the chart (under it in portrait), a launch-pace line works like an indicator with a price chart: an exponential moving average of launches (60-day time constant), annualized to launches per year and kicked up by every launch. Its gridlines mark one launch every 60, 30, 14 and 7 days, and its scale widens whenever the pace sets a new high. Launches that Epoch AI has not scored spray a low fountain on the date axis and show “—” instead of a number. The burst and its sound coincide with the release date; trajectories begin 0.36 seconds earlier. Each lab keeps its own burst form and marker shape: OpenAI, peony bursts and circles; Anthropic, glitter bursts and diamonds; Google, double-ring bursts and squares; DeepSeek, fan bursts and triangles; Meta, four-point star bursts and crosses. Where bursts crowd together only their glow and trails dim; each keeps its full size, rays and lifetime. A tag beside the newest burst names it with its score; launches a moment apart share one tag of at most three lines, which counts any further names towards the lists. Each lab's list shows its four newest names, newest on top, with release date and score: an arrival slides in and pushes older names down, and each name stays among the four newest for at least 1.4 seconds. An asterisk and hollow marker identify previews.

Capability scores

Burst heights use the Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) by Epoch AI, licensed CC BY 4.0, retrieved 2026-10-19 (CSV). 71 of 79 selected launches have a score. ECI combines many benchmarks onto one scale, and Epoch publishes an uncertainty interval several points wide for each model, so small gaps are not rankings. When an event groups several tiers, the table names the scored model. This visualization is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epoch AI.
